# Claim boundary

## Established by a passing run

- The contract admits exactly the 117 credit states in
  \(\{0,1,2\}^7\) satisfying both overlapping cap constraints.
- All \(3^7=2{,}187\) integer candidates are classified against the
  independently computed constraints.
- All 35 distinct four-member sets are accepted as candidate success sets.
- Every one of the 4,095 state--set quotes is at least 4 units.
- Every quote equals an independently computed residual-price sum.
- A quote and state-changing execution attain 4 units exactly.
- Every member can individually receive credit 2, so each marginal residual
  floor is zero even though the joint minimum is positive.
- Invalid caps, fractional credits, and duplicate member indices are rejected.

## Not established

- Ultimate beneficial ownership of pool funds.
- Attacker-independence of the pool controller or its funds.
- A production attack-cost lower bound.
- Organizational independence, availability, attribution, or deployment
  security.

The positive value is exact only inside the declared controlled provenance
scope.
